<spec>

## Intent
blossom is a free, open-source music player for Android (APK), Windows and Linux. It's built on BitChord's internals with pieces of Spotube v6, and it has a new liquid-glass interface that looks equally modern and polished on all three platforms.

## Goals
1. **One app, three platforms.** Android, Windows and Linux share one Kotlin/Compose Multiplatform codebase, with the same look and the same features.
2. **Liquid glass everywhere.** Frosted, softly refracting glass panels with a specular rim sit over an animated colour background drawn from the current album art.
3. **Streaming from YouTube Music** through BitChord's Innertube client, with no ads. Guest use is the default, and signing in to YouTube Music is optional (as in BitChord).
4. **Playlists.** Custom playlists, Liked Songs, and local-file playlists. A single playlist can mix local and streamed songs.
5. **Import** a single song or a whole playlist or album from **Spotify, YouTube Music/YouTube, SoundCloud and untitled.stream** links. Imports become normal, editable blossom playlists.
6. **Offline saving, Spotify-style.** Songs and playlists are cached **encrypted** inside blossom's private storage. They can only be played in blossom, can't be exported, and are removed on uninstall.
7. **Stats and Wrapped** for every week, month and year, covering listening on all synced devices.
8. **Automatic synced lyrics** from the same major sites BitChord uses, with an animation that looks genuinely beautiful.
9. **Sync without any blossom server:**
   - **B.** Sync through the user's own Google Drive, Dropbox, WebDAV, or any synced folder (such as Syncthing).
   - **C.** Device-to-device pairing on the same Wi-Fi with a QR code.
10. **Extras:**
    - crossfade and gapless playback
    - equalizer
    - sleep timer
    - Last.fm and ListenBrainz scrobbling
    - Discord Rich Presence (desktop)
    - media keys and lock-screen/system media controls on every platform
    - an update check against GitHub releases

## Non-goals
- iOS, macOS, web, Android TV, Wear OS
- video playback
- listen-together or jam sessions
- a public or third-party plugin system (sources sit behind internal interfaces instead)
- recommendations beyond YouTube Music's own home feed and radio
- blossom user accounts of any kind

## Anti-goals (it has failed if…)
- any platform looks or feels like a second-class port
- glass effects make scrolling, the player or lyrics stutter
- text on glass is hard to read
- it shows ads, has a paid tier or paid-only feature, or collects analytics or telemetry
- it needs a blossom account or a server someone has to pay for
- the offline cache can be copied out as playable plain audio files

## Hard rule: free for everyone
No blossom feature may require a paid account anywhere:
- **YouTube Music:** works as a guest.
- **Spotify:** public playlists import without logging in. Login is optional, for private playlists and Liked Songs.
- **untitled.stream:** works without signing in or with a free account. Its free plan includes unlimited listening.
- **Sync:** free tiers of Google Drive or Dropbox are enough. WebDAV, a synced folder and QR pairing cost nothing.

If any source turns out to need a paid membership to work, don't ship a paid-only path. Disable that source with a clear message, record the evidence under "Open questions for owner", and continue (see `<unattended_mode>`).

## Users and key flows
- **Play:** search → tap a song → mini player appears → open the full-screen player with glass controls and lyrics.
- **Import:**
  1. Paste a link, share it to blossom on Android, or open a deep link.
  2. blossom detects the source and lists the tracks with a live match status for each: ✓ matched, ? uncertain (still playable), ✗ unavailable.
  3. Save as a new playlist, or add to an existing one. The user can fix any match manually.
- **Offline:** use the "Save offline" toggle on a song, album or playlist. New songs later added to a saved playlist are saved automatically. Settings cover Wi-Fi only, a storage limit and quality.
- **Local files:**
  - Android: MediaStore scan, plus folders the user picks.
  - Desktop: folders the user picks, rescanned automatically when they change.
  - Formats: mp3, flac, m4a/aac, ogg, opus, wav.
  - Lyrics for local songs come from a sidecar `.lrc` file or embedded lyrics tags first, then online providers.
- **Stats:** a Stats tab with Week, Month and Year views. Wrapped is a story-style animated sequence that can be opened for any past or current period. Each card can be exported as an image.
- **Sync setup:** on the first device, pick a cloud or folder. On another device, pick the same cloud, or scan the first device's QR code.

## Behaviour details
- **Play counting:** a play counts after **30 seconds** of actual playback or when the song ends, whichever comes first. Listening time is the time actually played, including plays shorter than 30 seconds. Pauses and seeks add nothing.
- **Periods:** weeks run Monday to Sunday (ISO weeks), months and years are calendar periods, and all of them use the device's local time zone. A play belongs to the period in which it *started*.
- **Stats content per period:**
  - total listening minutes and counted plays
  - top 5 songs, top 5 artists and top 5 albums
  - listening by hour of day and by day of week
  - new artists discovered (first-ever counted play falls in the period)
  - longest daily streak within the period
  - comparison with the previous period, as a percentage change in listening time (none when the previous period is empty)
- **Lyrics fallback order:**
  1. word- or syllable-synced
  2. line-synced
  3. plain text
  4. "No lyrics found"
- **Lyrics animation:**
  - The active line scales up slightly on a spring and fills word by word.
  - Past and upcoming lines are dimmed and blurred by distance.
  - The view scrolls smoothly to keep the active line in place.
  - Tapping a line seeks to it.
  - A global "Reduce motion" setting turns off the springs, blur and animated background.
- **Import routing:**
  - **Spotify:** each track is matched against YouTube Music search.
  - **YouTube Music/YouTube links:** native, no matching needed.
  - **SoundCloud:** streams from SoundCloud directly. Falls back to YouTube Music matching only when the SoundCloud stream isn't playable (for example, preview-only Go+ tracks).
  - **untitled.stream:** see below. Never auto-matched.
- **Matching rules:**
  - Matching uses title, artist and duration, and produces a confidence score.
  - Only ✓ counts as matched, and a ✓ match needs the duration within ±3 s.
  - Low-confidence ? matches still play.
  - The user can replace any match from a search sheet.

## untitled.stream
untitled is where the owner gets Kanye yedits and unreleased songs uploaded by users. These tracks **do not exist on YouTube Music**, so they are never auto-matched there.
- **Direct streaming from untitled share links is the main path, and it's required.** blossom reproduces the requests untitled's own web player makes. Milestone 0 investigates this before any untitled code is written.
- **If untitled doesn't work without signing in, blossom adds a "Sign in to untitled" option.** This is required, not optional, whenever Task 0.3 finds that links, metadata or audio need an account.
  - **Where it appears:**
    - Settings → Accounts → untitled, with "Sign in" or "Signed in as <name> · Sign out"
    - an automatic prompt on the Import screen when an untitled link needs sign-in: "This untitled link needs you to sign in" with a **Sign in** button, after which the import resumes by itself
    - a "Sign in to play" action on untitled tracks that fail because the session expired
  - **How it works:** the user signs in through untitled's own login page shown inside blossom. That means an embedded web view: Android `WebView`, and on desktop a KMP web view such as `compose-webview-multiplatform` (KCEF/JCEF), bundled so users install nothing. Whatever untitled's login supports (email, magic link, Google/Apple) keeps working, and blossom never sees or stores the password.
  - **Alternative:** if Task 0.3 finds untitled has a simple email-code or password API that its own app uses, a native glass sign-in form is acceptable instead, as long as it handles every login method untitled offers.
  - **The session:**
    - Session cookies and tokens are stored in `SecureStore` on the device. They're never synced, logged or shared.
    - An expired session triggers the sign-in prompt again instead of an error.
    - Sign out clears the session.
  - **Free accounts only:** a free untitled account is enough. If streaming needs a paid membership, that's the paid-only stop condition below.
- **Imported untitled tracks are saved offline automatically** (setting, on by default), because these uploads are often taken down. They use the same encrypted cache.
- **When a link dies and the track wasn't saved,** the track stays in the playlist marked "unavailable". blossom retries it when the playlist is opened, at most once a day per track.
- **"Find replacement"** searches regular YouTube uploads (not YouTube Music) and SoundCloud, where re-uploads of leaks usually end up. It shows results, and **the user picks**. blossom never swaps a track in on its own.
- **Titles, uploader names and artwork** come from the share page and are kept exactly as uploaded (e.g. "Kanye West – Song (Yedit)"). No clean-up.
- **Stop conditions:**
  - If untitled audio is DRM-protected (EME/Widevine), don't try to get around it. Record the evidence.
  - If untitled only lets paid members stream, don't build a paid path. Record the evidence.
  - In either case, the untitled import shows "untitled links aren't supported yet" instead of breaking.

## Sync design
- **What syncs:**
  - playlists, their entries and their order
  - Liked Songs
  - play history (so stats cover all devices)
  - an allowlist of settings
  - local-file references, matched across devices by a content-derived ID; a song missing on the current device shows as "not on this device"
- **What never syncs:** cached audio, offline flags (saving offline is per device), secrets and tokens, device-specific settings (glass quality, folders, window size).
- **Change log:**
  - Every library change is an operation, stamped with a hybrid logical clock.
  - Operations are appended to the device's own log.
  - Each device writes only its own files, under `blossom/v1/devices/<deviceId>/`, in the sync location. Because no two devices ever write the same file, cloud apps and Syncthing never produce file conflicts.
- **Merging:**
  - Devices merge all logs deterministically.
  - Last writer wins per field. Play history is a union.
  - Playlist positions use fractional indexes, so concurrent inserts and moves never collide.
  - Deleting a playlist is final: later operations on that playlist are ignored.
- **QR pairing (C):**
  - Devices find each other on the local network and exchange the same logs directly.
  - The QR code carries a one-time pairing secret and the device's certificate fingerprint.
  - Peers relay each other's operations, so A↔B and B↔C means C ends up with A's changes.
- **Encryption:** sync data isn't encrypted beyond what the storage provider does, because it lives in the user's own storage.

<plan>

**Goal:** Ship blossom 1.0 for Android, Windows and Linux, implementing the whole spec above, in `velosako3/blossom`.

**Architecture:**
- A Gradle Kotlin Multiplatform project with `androidTarget()` and a `jvm("desktop")` target.
- `core/*` modules hold the domain models, database and operation log, design system, player and offline cache.
- `source/*` modules each implement one music service behind shared interfaces.
- `feature/*` modules hold the screens and their logic.
- `composeApp` wires everything together and holds the platform entry points.
- Every library change goes through an operation log from day one, so sync (Milestone 8) adds transport only and needs no retrofit.

<global_constraints>

  - The Android `applicationId` and root package are `io.github.velosako3.blossom`.

  - Every new source file starts with `// SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
  - Copied upstream files keep their original header and add `// Modified for blossom, <year>.` below it.
  - `NOTICE` lists every upstream project, its license and what was taken from it.
- **Strings:** user-facing strings live in Compose resources (`composeResources/values/strings.xml`), in English only for v1 but ready for translation. No hard-coded UI strings.
- **No telemetry, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.** Logs never contain tokens, cookies or full file paths outside debug builds.
- **Secrets:**
  - Secrets are never committed. `secrets.properties.example` documents every key, and builds without secrets still compile and run, with the affected features hidden.
  - Tokens are stored in Android Keystore-backed storage, or in the OS keyring on desktop (Windows Credential Manager, Linux Secret Service), with a 0600-permission file as the fallback.
- **Async and time:**
  - All network and disk I/O runs off the main thread on coroutines.
  - Times use `kotlinx.datetime.Instant` internally. Conversion to local time happens only at the presentation and stats boundary.
  - Inject a `Clock` and `TimeZone` wherever time matters, so tests can control them.
- **Tests:**
  - Shared logic is tested in `commonTest`, run via the desktop target (`./gradlew desktopTest`).
  - UI components and screens get Roborazzi screenshot tests rendered on desktop, with golden images committed under each module's `src/desktopTest/snapshots/`.
- **Live-network tests** are tagged and excluded by default. Run them with `-PliveTests=true`. Normal CI never hits the network: parsing is tested against sanitized fixtures recorded once.
- **Reduce motion:** every animation reads `LocalReduceMotion`. When true, springs become instant, blur effects tied to motion are off, and the background is static.
- **Glass quality:** all glass reads `LocalGlassQuality` (`FULL`, `REDUCED` or `OFF`) and must look intentional at each level, never broken.
</global_constraints>

<review_focus>
These are the failure modes most likely to bite real users. Each one has a test in the task that owns it.

1. **Stream URL expiry mid-play or after a long pause.** YouTube stream URLs expire. On HTTP 403/410, or when expired, re-resolve transparently and resume at the same position with no error shown. Owned by Task 2.3.
2. **No network.** The app opens straight to the library. Saved songs play. Unsaved songs are greyed out and skipped in the queue. Imports, scrobbles and sync queue up and retry later. There are no endless spinners or crashes. Owned by Tasks 5.4 and 6.1.
3. **Huge imports and process death.** A 5,000-track playlist imports with a responsive UI. If the app is killed partway through, the import resumes where it stopped, with no duplicate entries. Owned by Task 6.1.
4. **Concurrent offline edits and clock skew.** The same playlist edited on two offline devices, or a device with a wrong clock, merges deterministically with no lost or duplicated entries. Owned by Tasks 3.1, 3.2 and 8.2.
5. **Time zones, DST and period boundaries in stats.**
   - Sunday 23:59 versus Monday 00:00 in Europe/London.
   - A DST-change week.
   - ISO week 53 of 2026.
   - A play spanning midnight is attributed to its start time.

   Owned by Task 7.2.
</review_focus>

  - Score = 0.5 × title similarity (normalized Levenshtein) + 0.35 × artist overlap (Jaccard on normalized names) + 0.15 × duration score.
  - Duration score: 1.0 when the difference is ≤ 3 s, falling linearly to 0 at 10 s. If the duration is unknown, it counts 0.5.
  - Status:
    - MATCHED when the score is ≥ 0.80 **and** the duration difference is ≤ 3 s (or the duration is unknown)
    - UNCERTAIN when the score is 0.55–0.80, or ≥ 0.80 with the duration difference over 3 s
    - UNAVAILABLE below 0.55
  - If the source gives an ISRC and a candidate's ISRC matches, it's MATCHED with confidence 1.0.
- **Job runner:**
  - Jobs and items are persisted (`import_job`, `import_job_item`).
  - 4 items are matched at once, each with 3 retries.
  - Entries are written in batches of 50 through `LibraryCommands.addEntries`, keeping the source order.
  - `resumePending()` runs on app start (review focus 3).
  - Native tracks (`ImportedTrack.nativeTrack != null`) skip matching and are MATCHED.
  - `UpdateExisting` adds only tracks not already present (by origin track key) and never removes the user's edits. This powers "Refresh from source".
